The problem

More tools. Less togetherness.

Teams have never been more connected, or more fragmented. Attention splits across channels, meetings multiply, and the quiet people stop speaking. Confidence erodes without anyone deciding it should.

Confidence is a team practice.

Work with me

Three ways to work together. One for larger organizations.

For one leader

Personal Advisory

Private, confidential work with Dr. Castellan over twelve weeks. Sharpen how you lead, decide and show up when pressure is high.

  • ◆Twelve weeks, one-to-one
  • ◆Weekly 60-minute sessions
  • ◆Between-session notes and practice

For a leadership team

Team Working Sessions

A series of facilitated sessions for six to ten leaders who need to move together. Build shared habits that survive a busy calendar.

  • ◆Six sessions over three months
  • ◆On site in Boston or New York, or live online
  • ◆A team agreement you actually use

For a small cohort

The Castellan Roundtable

A peer cohort of eight to twelve leaders from different companies. Candid conversation, practical frameworks, and a confidential circle.

  • ◆Quarterly cohorts, application only
  • ◆Two live intensives plus monthly sessions
  • ◆Peer circle that continues after the program

Questions

Before you reach out

What is a first conversation like?+

It is a free 30-minute call. We talk about what you are facing, and I tell you honestly whether and how I can help. There is no sales presentation.

How do I know which format fits?+

Personal advisory suits a single leader. Team sessions suit a group that works together every day. The Roundtable suits leaders who want peers from other companies. If you are unsure, start with the call.

Do you work with whole organizations?+

Yes. Larger engagements begin with a briefing, then a scoped diagnostic, and are usually covered by an NDA and a master services agreement.

Where do you work?+

I teach in New York and consult with teams in Boston. Sessions run on site in either city or live online.

Is the work confidential?+

Yes. Personal sessions and Roundtable conversations are confidential by agreement. Organizational work follows the confidentiality terms you set.
